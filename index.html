DILATEd Cardiomyopathy Survival Score (DILATESS/DILATESS-LGE)

Integer score calculator for dilated cardiomyopathy all-cause mortality prediction (1–5 years). Single-file, no backend Custom domain: dilatess-score.online

The prediction model was developed in the DEMAND study (Deep phenotypic Multimodality-based Analysis of Dilated Cardiomyopathy), a prospective Chinese DCM cohort at West China Hospital, Sichuan University.
