DILATEd Cardiomyopathy Survival Score

DILATESS / DILATESS-LGE calculator for predicting 1–5-year all-cause mortality in dilated cardiomyopathy, with or without late gadolinium enhancement (LGE) information.
Developed in the DEMAND study (Deep phenotypic Multimodality-based Analysis of Dilated Cardiomyopathy), a prospective Chinese DCM cohort at West China Hospital, Sichuan University.
